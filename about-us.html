About us

Why Examindex exists, and how it works.

Examindex is a free study tool built by a 6th year student who wanted a faster, more efficient way to prepare for the Leaving Certificate. Instead of trawling through scattered PDFs across different sites, Examindex brings past exam papers together in one place and organises them by subject, level, and topic — so revision time goes into studying, not searching.

Every paper on this site comes from real past Leaving Certificate exams, set and published by the State Examinations Commission (SEC). Examindex categorises these papers and marking schemes by subject, level, and topic, so it's faster to find and practise the exact type of question you're revising, rather than working through an entire paper to get to one section.

Examindex did not create, and does not claim to have created, any of the exam papers or marking schemes hosted on this site — all original material belongs to the SEC. Examindex hosts its own copies of these files rather than linking through to examinations.ie, so that papers stay reliably accessible and can be organised in a more useful way for revision. Examindex is a study resource built by a student, not an official SEC service, and is in no way affiliated with the State Examinations Commission.

This project is actively being built and improved. See What's new? for a running log of updates, and get in touch via Contact us with feedback, corrections, or ideas for what to add next.
