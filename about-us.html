ABOUT EXAMINDEX

A better way to find past papers.

examindex is a focused study archive: fewer distractions, clearer organisation, faster access to the question you actually want.

examindex was built to solve a simple problem: past Leaving Certificate papers are useful, but finding the right question inside a pile of PDFs can take longer than the revision itself.

The site organises past papers by subject, level, paper and topic. That means you can revise a specific area — rather than opening full papers at random and hunting through them.

The exam papers and marking schemes are original examination material published by the State Examinations Commission. examindex categorises and presents that material as a study resource; it is not an official SEC service and is not affiliated with the SEC.

The project is intentionally account-free. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to configure before you can study.
