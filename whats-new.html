Two new pages explaining what Examindex is, why it exists, and how to get in touch with feedback or corrections.

Buttons, cards, tabs and panels across the site now use rounded corners instead of sharp edges.

Batch download, with year filtering

A new Batch Download page lets you tick several papers by subject, level, type and year, then download them all together as a single zip file. The manifest that powers it now rebuilds itself automatically whenever a paper is added.

Home and What's new moved into a slide-out sidebar, alongside a new Generate a Test page. A Light/Dark appearance toggle was also added, with your choice remembered between visits.

Each subject page now has a Past Papers tool — pick a year, level, and exam paper or marking scheme, and go straight to the file.

The archive has launched with folders for English, Irish and Maths, each split into Ordinary and Higher level. Papers are added as they're filed — check back here for updates.

Every subject page now has two level tabs so you can go straight to the papers you're revising for.
